What venue software does, and what it doesn't

This is the capability map for venue management software: every feature our competitor research has turned up, grouped by the job it does, with who has it and where the gaps are. Use it to see what Tawn must match and where it can stand out.

One competitor so far. Every column here is Strikee, so read "the market" as "Strikee" until more profiles are added. The page grows a column per competitor when it is regenerated. For Strikee in depth, read Strikee, explained.

The short version

  • Most of the counter is covered. Sessions, pricing, split bills, credit and the cashbook all work in Strikee's app.
  • The gaps sit where money leaves the venue's control: closing the till, refunds, taking payment online, and working without a connection.
  • Most of the evidence is first-hand. Most cells were checked in the product, not read off a website.

Where the openings are

Every capability that isn't a plain "yes", with a note on what Tawn could do. The notes are opinions; everything else comes from the matrix.

Table stakes

What a venue will expect on day one, because the competitor already does it. Tawn needs these, or a clear reason to leave them out.

For Tawn

Two of these sit only in Strikee's top tier: prepaid memberships and the public venue page. Putting them in Tawn's base plan is a cheap way to look generous on price.
